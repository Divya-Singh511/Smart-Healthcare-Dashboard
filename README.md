Smart Healthcare Dashboard

Project Overview
The Smart Healthcare Dashboard is a data visualization project developed using Tableau Public. It analyzes chronic diseases such as heart disease, diabetes, and stroke to understand disease prevalence and associated health factors.

Objectives
Analyze the prevalence of heart disease, diabetes, and stroke.

Explore disease patterns across age groups and BMI categories.

Study lifestyle factors such as smoking, alcohol consumption, and exercise.

Analyze clinical measurements such as cholesterol and glucose.

Explore symptoms associated with chronic diseases.

Support healthcare data understanding and early risk assessment.

Dataset 
The dataset contains 5,000 records and 16 columns related to demographics, clinical measurements, lifestyle factors, disease indicators, and symptoms.
